1. Personal Productivity & Utilities

Enhance individual efficiency, organization, or device functionality.

  • Task Management: Todoist, Trello
  • Note-Taking: Evernote, Notion
  • Calendar: Google Calendar, Fantastical
  • File Management: Dropbox, Google Drive
  • Utilities: 1Password, NordVPN

Relevance: Journal or task tracker using Firebase + LocalStorage

2. Communication & Social Networking

Connecting people through messaging, calls, or social platforms.

  • Messaging: WhatsApp, Telegram
  • Social Media: Instagram, LinkedIn
  • Video Conferencing: Zoom, Teams
  • Dating: Tinder, Bumble
  • Community: Discord, Reddit

Relevance: Geo-Story for real-time, map-based sharing

3. Entertainment & Media

Audiovisual content and interactive experiences.

  • Streaming: Netflix, Spotify
  • Gaming: Among Us, Candy Crush
  • Reading: Kindle, Audible
  • Editing: Canva, Adobe Premiere Rush
  • Live Events: Twitch, YouTube Live

Relevance: Mini Arcade or Fitness Quest using GameEngine

4. Education & Learning

Support for skill development and training.

  • E-Learning: Coursera, Duolingo
  • Reference: Wikipedia, Merriam-Webster
  • Tutoring: Khan Academy, Chegg
  • Language: Babbel, Rosetta Stone
  • Kids: ABCmouse, Toca Boca

Relevance: Snap & Solve with Camera + APIs

5. Health & Fitness

Promoting physical or mental well-being.

  • Fitness: Fitbit, Strava
  • Mental Health: Calm, Headspace
  • Nutrition: MyFitnessPal, Lifesum
  • Medical: WebMD, Ada
  • Sleep: Sleep Cycle, Relax Melodies

Relevance: Fitness Quest and MoodSpace

6. Lifestyle & Personalization

Enhance life or reflect personal expression.

  • Home: Houzz, Google Home
  • Fashion: ASOS, Sephora
  • Hobbies: Pinterest, PlantSnap
  • Personalization: Zedge, Nova Launcher
  • Events: Eventbrite, Paperless Post

Relevance: MoodSpace for emotional well-being

7. Travel & Navigation

Exploration and location-based tools.

  • Navigation: Google Maps, Waze
  • Planning: Tripadvisor, Expedia
  • Local Guides: Yelp, Foursquare
  • Transport: Uber, Citymapper
  • Translation: Google Translate, iTranslate

Relevance: Geo-Story, Local Treasure using GPS/Maps

8. Finance & Money Management

Managing personal or business finances.

  • Banking: Revolut, Chase
  • Budgeting: YNAB, Mint
  • Investing: Coinbase, Robinhood
  • Payments: PayPal, Venmo
  • Accounting: TurboTax, QuickBooks

Relevance: Budgeting app possible with Firebase

9. Shopping & E-Commerce

Buying, selling, and discovering products.

  • Retail: Amazon, eBay
  • Fashion: Zara, Depop
  • Grocery: Instacart, Shipt
  • Deals: Honey, Rakuten
  • Secondhand: Poshmark, Mercari

Relevance: Local market app with GPS/Images possible

10. Business & Professional Tools

Support for professional workflows.

  • Collaboration: Slack, Asana
  • CRM: Salesforce, HubSpot
  • HR: LinkedIn, Gusto
  • Analytics: Tableau, Google Analytics
  • Industry Tools: Epic, Square

Relevance: Team task app possible but not primary focus

11. News & Information

Current events and niche content.

  • Aggregators: Flipboard, Google News
  • Magazines: NYT, The Economist
  • Weather: AccuWeather, Dark Sky
  • Sports: ESPN, Bleacher Report
  • Niche: Feedly, Pocket

Relevance: Local news app with GPS + Firebase possible

12. Augmented & Virtual Reality

Immersive interaction and visualization.

  • AR Games: Pokémon GO, Wizards Unite
  • AR Utilities: IKEA Place, Google Lens
  • VR Experiences: Oculus, VRChat
  • Training: Simulations for health, mechanics

Relevance: Local Treasure with basic AR features

13. Kids & Family

Apps for young users and families.

  • Learning Games: PBS Kids, Duplo World
  • Storytelling: Storyboard, Epic!
  • Parental Tools: Family Link, Cozi
  • Creativity: Kids Paint, Toca Lab

Relevance: Adapt Local Treasure for scavenger hunt

14. Food & Drink

Dining, cooking, and food services.

  • Recipes: Tasty, Yummly
  • Delivery: Uber Eats, DoorDash
  • Meal Planning: Paprika, Mealime
  • Reviews: Zomato, OpenTable

Relevance: Recipe app using Camera + Firebase possible

15. Government & Civic

Public services and civic engagement.

  • Services: DMV, IRS2Go
  • Voting: BallotReady, Vote.org
  • Community: Nextdoor, SeeClickFix
  • Emergency: FEMA, Red Cross

Relevance: Local issue-reporting with GPS/Images

Observations & Notes

  • Overlap: Apps may span multiple categories. Choose a focus.
  • Emerging Trends: Growth in AR/VR, mental health, and civic apps.
  • Course Fit: Projects align with Health, EdTech, Entertainment, Travel, AR.
  • Market: Gaming, Social, Productivity dominate stores.
  • Scope: MVPs in Productivity or Education suit short sprints.
